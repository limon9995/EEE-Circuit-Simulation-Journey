# 🎓 Student Academic Access — OrCAD X & PSpice

This guide explains how EEE students can obtain **Cadence academic access** for learning circuit design and simulation with **OrCAD X and PSpice**.

The purpose of this repository is to document a practical, student-friendly workflow that can be followed by other EEE students.

---

## 📌 What is OrCAD X?

**OrCAD X** is an electronic design automation (EDA) platform from **Cadence**.

It can be used for:

* Schematic circuit design
* Electronic circuit development
* PSpice simulation
* Circuit analysis
* Simulation result visualization
* PCB design workflows

For this learning journey, our main focus will be **circuit drawing, analysis, and PSpice simulation**.

---

## 🌐 Official Cadence Resources

Before downloading or installing anything, always use official Cadence resources.

* **Cadence Official Website:** https://www.cadence.com/
* **Cadence Academic Network:** https://www.cadence.com/en_US/home/academic-program.html
* **Cadence Downloads:** https://downloads.cadence.com/

> ⚠️ Avoid downloading OrCAD/PSpice from unofficial websites. Use official Cadence sources whenever possible.

# 🎓 Academic Access Process

The general process looks like this:

```text
University / Student Email
          ↓
Cadence Academic Registration
          ↓
Student Verification
          ↓
Academic License / Product Access
          ↓
OrCAD X Installation
          ↓
PSpice Circuit Simulation
```

---

## 1️⃣ Use Your University Student Email

Start with your valid university/student email address.

Cadence may use the academic email and university information to verify that you are a student.

### You may need:

* University name
* Student email
* Student information
* Academic information

Use your own real academic information during registration.

---

## 2️⃣ Create / Access Your Cadence Account

Visit the official Cadence website and follow the academic access process.

After registration, complete any verification steps requested by Cadence.

🔗 **Cadence Academic Program:**
https://www.cadence.com/en_US/home/academic-program.html

The exact registration process may change over time, so always follow the current instructions provided by Cadence.

---

## 3️⃣ Complete Academic Verification

After submitting your academic information, Cadence may verify your student status.

Once verification is completed, eligible students may receive access to academic software and licensing information.

The available products and access period can vary depending on the current Cadence academic program.

---

## 4️⃣ Check Your Cadence Email

After successful registration/verification, check the email address associated with your Cadence account.

Cadence may send information regarding:

* Product access
* License information
* Download instructions
* Installation instructions
* Account information

📌 **Important:** Keep license-related information private.

# 🧠 Why Are We Using OrCAD X & PSpice?

The goal of this project is not just to learn software.

As EEE students, we want to develop the ability to move from a theoretical circuit to a verified simulation.

Our basic workflow will be:

```text
Understand the Circuit
        ↓
Draw the Circuit
        ↓
Calculate Manually
        ↓
Simulate with PSpice
        ↓
Compare Results
        ↓
Find Errors
        ↓
Debug
        ↓
Verify
```

This workflow will be used throughout the entire repository.
