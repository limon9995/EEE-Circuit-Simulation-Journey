
# 💻 Download OrCAD X

This guide explains how to download **OrCAD X** for the EEE Circuit Simulation Journey.

The software should always be downloaded from an official Cadence source.

---

## 🎯 Goal

We will use OrCAD X for:

- Circuit schematic drawing
- PSpice simulation
- Circuit analysis
- Simulation result visualization
- EEE laboratory and assignment preparation

---

## 🌐 Official Cadence Download

### Cadence Downloads

🔗 https://downloads.cadence.com/

Use the official Cadence download portal whenever possible.

---

## 📧 Download Link From Your License Email

After completing the Cadence academic registration and receiving a product/license email, Cadence may provide a direct installer link.

For example, an OrCAD X installer may be provided as:

`OrCADXInstall.exe`

### Important

The exact download link and available products can change over time.

Always use the download link provided by your own valid Cadence account/license email or the official Cadence download portal.

⚠️ Avoid Unofficial Downloads

Do not download OrCAD X from:

Cracked software websites
Torrent websites
Random file-hosting websites
Unverified Google Drive links
Unknown third-party download sites

Using unofficial software can create security, licensing, and installation problems.
